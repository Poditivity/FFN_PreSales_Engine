# Modal

A decision that has to be made before anything else continues. Nothing else.

## Rules

- Use it for confirmation of something destructive or irreversible, and for a short focused form of three fields or fewer. Anything longer is a page or a drawer.
- The title is the question, ending in a question mark: "Mark this lead as lost?"
- The body states the consequence in plain terms, including what is kept and what can be undone. FFN's register: no contractions, no reassurance that is not true.
- Two actions, right-aligned: cancel on the left as `ghost`, the committing action on the right. A destructive commit is `danger` and its label repeats the verb from the title.
- Escape and a click on the scrim both cancel. The committing action is never the default focus.
- One modal at a time. A modal never opens another modal.

## The consumer provides

Open state, focus trap and restore, escape and scrim handling, and the action handlers. This component renders inline; portal it in the application.
